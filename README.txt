See at a glance whether your pets are inside or outside, when they were last seen, and how much they ate and drank today. Smart pet doors, feeders and water fountains show their battery level and what is left in the bowls.

Decide who comes and goes by changing the lock mode of your pet doors from Homey or from a Flow. Start a Flow when a pet comes home, when a battery runs low or when an unknown chip tries to get in. Log in with your Sure Petcare account in the app settings to get started.
